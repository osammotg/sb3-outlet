# SB3 Outlet Website Redesign Notes

## Goal
Complete redesign of SB3 Outlet website, copying Jack's Surfboards (https://jackssurfboards.com) as the primary design reference. Disregard the current site design -- start fresh.

## Phase 2: Regenerate images using AI to make them original

## Design Inspirations & Sections to Copy

### From Jack's Surfboards (https://jackssurfboards.com) -- PRIMARY REFERENCE
- **Hero section**: Full-bleed lifestyle photography, warm golden tones, editorial feel with models. Handwritten script font for campaigns ("Safari Summer"). Magazine-quality styling.
- **"All Surf" photo grid section**: We LOVE this. Mosaic-style grid with lifestyle photos -- surfer with board, Xero Gravity surfboards spotlight, fins, wetsuits. Beautiful color-matched images with overlay text. Section reads "All surf shop, all the surf you need." The photos really slap and have cool matching colors.
- **"Shop By Category" cards**: Large photo cards for Wetsuits (219 items), Surfboards (214 items), Surfboard Fins (266 items), Surf Leashes. Each card has a lifestyle photo background with category name + item count overlay.
- **Sale banner section**: Bold typography, collage-style layout with multiple product photos (men's sale, women's sale, snow sale). High energy.
- **Brand logos bar**: RVCA, Billabong, Vans, Rip Curl, Salty Crew -- clean horizontal row on white.
- **"Your Favorites" product carousel**: Tabbed (Kids, Mens, Womens, Best Sellers) with product cards.
- **Store locations section**: Vintage photo of storefront with nostalgic feel.
- **Overall aesthetic**: Warm palette (sand, gold, cream), editorial lifestyle photography, clean minimal nav, feels like a lifestyle brand not just a shop.

### From Surf Style (https://surfstyle.com)
- **Video hero**: They use a looping video in the hero section instead of a static image. The video shows beach/surf lifestyle footage. This makes the site feel alive and dynamic. We want this for SB3.
- Video source: Shopify CDN MP4 format.

## Sections for New SB3 Outlet Website (in order)

1. **Nav** -- Minimal, clean, Jack's style
2. **Hero** -- Video background (like Surf Style) with lifestyle footage + campaign text overlay
3. **"All Surf" Photo Grid** -- Mosaic layout with product/lifestyle images (surfboards, wetsuits, fins, accessories). Copy Jack's layout.
4. **Shop By Category** -- Large photo cards for each category (Surf, Skate, Snow, Clothing, Shoes, Accessories)
5. **Brands Bar** -- Logo row of all carried brands
6. **Featured Products / Your Favorites** -- Product carousel with category tabs
7. **Google Reviews** -- Keep the reviews section we just built
8. **Store Locations** -- Both shops with maps
9. **Footer** -- Clean, minimal
